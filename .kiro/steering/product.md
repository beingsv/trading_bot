# Product Overview

AI-powered intraday trading bot for Indian stock markets (NSE via AngelOne API). The system learns and adapts trading strategies through paper trading with virtual capital.

## Core Capabilities

- Multi-factor market analysis (technical indicators, news sentiment, FII/DII flows, global markets)
- Self-learning strategy system that evolves based on performance
- Paper trading simulation with ₹1,00,000 virtual capital
- Real-time buy/sell/hold signal generation
- Risk management with automatic stop-loss (2%) and take-profit (5%)
- Market condition detection (bull/bear/sideways/high volatility)

## Key Principles

- Safety first: Paper trading mode by default
- Learn from every trade: Continuous feedback loop
- Multi-source consensus: Weighted signals from strategies (50%), technical (30%), sentiment (20%)
- Risk-controlled: Max 10% capital per position
- Adaptive: Strategies adjust to market conditions
